About

Welcome, I am a games writer and concept artist using the title of “The Volto Corporation” to publish my projects.

I love writing emotional stories full of deep connections with characters the player meets along their journey. I enjoy writing romance, fiction, psychological horror & fantasy. My art focuses on characters and terrifying creatures to unnerve and keep you one edge.

I hope that my art or writing inspires you to try something creative yourself.

Projects

Famine Of Flesh

You are alone. The world has ended. There is no hope.

You keep on living. You believe somewhere and somehow you’ll find someone. You keep on walking.

“Famine of Flesh” is a survival horror romance visual novel. You are forced to survive in a new apocalyptic world taken over by the dead. You travel this new world by yourself until meeting the few survivors that are left. Survive the horrors of the new world and form strong connections with your companions either romantic or platonic.
